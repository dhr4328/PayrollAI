# Leave Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the official leave policy for all employees covered under our payroll system. Leave entitlements are governed by the Factories Act, Shops & Establishments Act, and applicable state regulations.

---

## Types of Leave

### 1. Casual Leave (CL)
- **Entitlement:** 12 days per calendar year
- **Eligibility:** All confirmed employees
- **Maximum at a time:** 3 consecutive days (beyond which Earned Leave applies)
- **Carry Forward:** Not allowed. Lapses at year-end.
- **Notice Required:** Prior notice required except in emergencies
- **Encashment:** Not applicable

### 2. Earned Leave / Privilege Leave (PL/EL)
- **Accrual Rate:** 1 day for every 20 days worked
- **Minimum Eligibility:** 240 working days in a year
- **Maximum Accumulation:** 60 days (balance above 60 lapses)
- **Carry Forward:** Up to 30 days per year
- **Encashment:** Allowed at resignation/retirement (subject to management approval)
- **Notice Required:** Minimum 7 days advance notice for 3+ days

### 3. Sick Leave (SL)
- **Entitlement:** 10 days per calendar year
- **Eligibility:** All employees from date of joining
- **Medical Certificate:** Required for leave exceeding 2 consecutive days
- **Carry Forward:** Not allowed
- **Encashment:** Not applicable

### 4. Maternity Leave
- **Duration:** 26 weeks (for first 2 children), 12 weeks thereafter
- **Eligibility:** Female employees with 80+ days of service in preceding 12 months
- **Applicable Law:** Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (as amended 2017)
- **Pay:** Full salary during leave period
- **Notice:** Required at least 8 weeks in advance where possible

### 5. Paternity Leave
- **Duration:** 5 working days
- **Eligibility:** Male employees within 15 days of child's birth
- **Pay:** Full salary

### 6. Public Holidays (PH)
- As per applicable state government notifications (typically 10-15 days per year)
- List declared at the beginning of each calendar year

### 7. Weekly Off
- 1 day per week (Sunday or rotational as per department schedule)
- Factory workers: As per shift schedule

---

## Leave Application Process

1. Apply through the HR management system (or supervisor) at least 3 days in advance for planned leave
2. Emergency leave: Inform supervisor by phone/message on the same day
3. Leave form must be submitted on return for all types except Public Holiday
4. Leave not sanctioned in advance may be treated as Loss of Pay (LOP)

---

## Leave During Probation

- Probationers are eligible for Sick Leave and Emergency Casual Leave only
- Earned Leave accrual begins after confirmation

---

## Loss of Pay (LOP)

- Absence beyond sanctioned leave entitlement is treated as Loss of Pay
- LOP deduction: (Per Day Rate × Number of LOP Days)
- Excess absence without approval may lead to disciplinary action

---

## Leave Encashment

- Earned Leave encashment: Maximum 30 days per year during service
- On resignation: Full earned leave balance is encashed
- On retirement: Full earned leave balance (up to 300 days lifetime cap) is encashed

---

## Important Notes

- Leave records are maintained by HR and are final
- Leave cannot be taken in advance of accrual
- Any misrepresentation of leave reason may lead to disciplinary action
- Leave salary = Basic + Allowances (not including OT or incentives)
