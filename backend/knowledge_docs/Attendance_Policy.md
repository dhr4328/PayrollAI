# Attendance Policy

## Overview

This policy defines attendance requirements, working hours, marking procedures, late marks, and absence management for all employees.

---

## Working Hours

| Category | Standard Hours |
|---|---|
| General Shift | 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (8 hours + 1 hour break) |
| Factory Shift A | 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM (8 hours) |
| Factory Shift B | 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM (8 hours) |
| Factory Shift C | 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM (8 hours) |
| Security | 12-hour shifts (rotational) |

---

## Attendance Marking

- **Biometric/Card Punch:** Mandatory in and out punching for all employees
- **Manual Muster Roll:** For contract workers and field employees
- **Supervisor Override:** In case of technical failure, supervisor signs the muster
- **Attendance Cut-off:** 24th of each month for payroll inclusion

---

## Paid Days Calculation

```
Paid Days = Present Days + Public Holidays + Weekly Off Days
```

- **Present Days:** Days the employee physically reported to work
- **Public Holidays (PH):** Declared holidays where employee need not report
- **Weekly Off:** 1 day per week (counted as paid)
- **Absent Days:** Not included in paid days → Loss of Pay applies

---

## Grace Period

- **Grace time:** 10 minutes after shift start time
- **Late mark:** Arrival more than 10 minutes after start time
- **Late mark deduction:** 3 late marks = 1 half-day LOP
- **Habitual lateness (>5 late marks/month):** May lead to written warning

---

## Absent Without Leave (AWOL)

- Absence without prior approval and without informing supervisor
- AWOL is treated as Loss of Pay (LOP)
- 3 consecutive AWOL days: Disciplinary notice issued
- 7+ consecutive AWOL days: May be treated as voluntary abandonment of service

---

## Loss of Pay (LOP)

- **Calculation:** Per Day Rate × Number of LOP Days
- **Deducted from:** Monthly net payable salary
- **LOP Report:** Available in payroll system for HR review
- **Rounding:** Half-day absence = 0.5 LOP day

---

## Public Holidays

- List declared at start of each calendar year by HR
- Typically includes: Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali, Christmas, Eid, Holi, and applicable state holidays
- **Working on PH:** Compensated with double pay or comp off (see Overtime Policy)

---

## Attendance Monitoring

### HR Responsibilities
- Generate daily attendance report
- Track absenteeism trends by department
- Issue notices for repeat offenders
- Share attendance data with payroll team by 24th each month

### Supervisor Responsibilities
- Mark absent/present accurately in system
- Approve/reject leave applications within 24 hours
- Notify HR of any discrepancies

---

## Attendance During Probation

- Probationers are expected to maintain ≥95% attendance
- Attendance below 85% in any month may lead to extension of probation
- More than 3 unauthorized absences may lead to termination

---

## Long Absence (Medical / Other)

- Long medical absence (>10 days): Medical certificate required from registered doctor
- Extensions beyond Sick Leave entitlement: Treated as LOP (or Earned Leave if available)
- **Leave Without Pay (LWP):** Formal sanctioned absence without salary, must be applied and approved by HR Head

---

## Attendance Reports Available in System

- Daily Attendance Summary
- Monthly Attendance Register (Muster Roll)
- Absent Employees List
- Late Arrival Report
- Department-wise Attendance Summary
