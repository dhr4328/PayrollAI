# Overtime Policy

## Overview

This policy governs overtime work eligibility, limits, approval process, and compensation for all employees.

---

## Definition of Overtime

Overtime is defined as work performed beyond the standard working hours in any given day or week:
- **Standard Daily Hours:** 8 hours (1 shift)
- **Standard Weekly Hours:** 48 hours (6 days × 8 hours)
- Work exceeding these hours qualifies as overtime

---

## Eligibility for Overtime

### Eligible Categories
- All **workmen/daily wage employees** under the Factories Act
- Contract workers engaged through licensed contractors
- Employees in production, security, maintenance, and logistics roles

### Non-Eligible Categories
- Employees in managerial or supervisory roles (above the threshold defined by Factories Act)
- Administrative staff who are exempt under applicable law
- Employees on fixed compensation (Fixed Pay category)

---

## Overtime Rate

### Standard Overtime Pay Formula

```
Extra Pay = (Per Day Rate ÷ 8) × Extra Duty Hours
```

**Example:**
- Per Day Rate: ₹800
- Extra Duty Hours: 4
- Extra Pay = (₹800 ÷ 8) × 4 = **₹400**

### Holiday/National Holiday Overtime
- On declared public holidays or national holidays: **Double the standard OT rate**
- Formula: `Extra Pay × 2`

---

## Maximum Overtime Limits

As per the **Factories Act, 1948 (Section 64)**:
- Maximum overtime permitted: **50 hours per quarter** (with government exemption)
- No individual employee should be required to work more than **10 hours in a day** (including OT)
- Exemptions for urgent repair work or special government orders

**Company Policy (Stricter):**
- Maximum overtime: **3 hours per day**
- Maximum per month: **30 hours per month** per employee
- Exceptions require written approval from factory manager + HR Head

---

## Overtime Approval Process

1. **Supervisor initiates:** Identifies need for overtime and estimates hours required
2. **Department Head approval:** Must be obtained before assigning OT
3. **HR Records:** All OT hours must be recorded in the attendance register
4. **Employee consent:** Employees cannot be forced to work OT; must be voluntary
5. **Cut-off for payroll:** OT hours reported by the 20th of the month are included in that month's payroll

---

## Recording Overtime

- Overtime hours are recorded in the system as **Extra Duty Hours**
- HR or supervisor must update the payroll system before payroll processing cut-off
- Muster Roll entry is mandatory per Factories Act requirements

---

## Overtime Register (Form IV)

Under the Factories Act, a **Register of Overtime** must be maintained:
- Employee name and code
- Date of overtime
- Hours worked overtime
- Compensation paid
- Available via the payroll system as **Form XXII** / Overtime Report

---

## Compensatory Off (Comp Off)

In lieu of monetary overtime compensation, management may offer Compensatory Off:
- **1 CL** credited for every 8 hours of overtime worked
- Must be availed within 30 days
- Requires prior agreement between employee and management
- Not applicable for all categories — check with HR

---

## Tax Treatment of Overtime

- Overtime pay is included in gross salary for tax calculation purposes
- PF is NOT deducted on overtime pay (as it is not part of basic wage)
- ESI is deducted on total gross wages including overtime (if gross ≤ ₹21,000)
- Professional Tax is calculated on total payable salary including OT

---

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

- Paying below the mandated overtime rate is a violation of the Factories Act
- Penalty: Fine + potential prosecution under Section 92 of the Factories Act
- All OT payments must be through official payroll (no cash payments)
