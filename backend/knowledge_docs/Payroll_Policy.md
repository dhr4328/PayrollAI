# Payroll Policy

## Overview

This document describes the payroll processing policy, statutory compliance rules, salary components, and deduction mechanisms applicable to all employees.

---

## Payroll Processing Cycle

- **Payroll Period:** Monthly (1st to last day of the month)
- **Processing Date:** Payroll processed between 25th-28th of each month
- **Salary Credit Date:** Last working day of the month (or 1st of next month if last day is holiday)
- **Cut-off for Attendance:** 24th of the month

---

## Salary Components

### Earnings
| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Basic / Per Day Rate | Base rate × Paid Days |
| Extra Pay (OT) | (Per Day Rate / 8) × Extra Duty Hours |
| Bin Card Amount | Production/incentive bonus |
| Difference Amount | Arrears or adjustments |
| Total Earning | Sum of all earnings minus hour deductions |

### Deductions
| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Employee PF (EE PF) | 12% of Basic (capped at ₹15,000 wage ceiling) |
| Employee ESI (EE ESI) | 0.75% of Gross (if gross ≤ ₹21,000) |
| Professional Tax (PT) | ₹200/month (if gross > ₹12,000) |
| Labour Welfare Fund (LWF) | As per state schedule |
| Other Deductions | Loans, advances, etc. |
| Mediclaim | Group health insurance premium |
| Shoes/Uniform | Recovery deduction if applicable |

---

## Provident Fund (PF) Rules

- **Applicable Law:** Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952
- **Employee Contribution:** 12% of Basic/Payable Wage
- **Employer Contribution:** 13% of Basic/Payable Wage
  - 3.67% → EPF (Employees' Provident Fund)
  - 8.33% → EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme, capped at ₹15,000)
  - 0.5% → EDLI (Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance)
  - 0.5% → EPF Admin Charges
- **Wage Ceiling for PF:** ₹15,000/month (contributions capped at this wage)
- **Eligibility:** All employees earning ≤ ₹15,000/month (compulsory); above this, optional on mutual agreement

---

## Employee State Insurance (ESI) Rules

- **Applicable Law:** Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948
- **Employee Contribution:** 0.75% of Gross Wages
- **Employer Contribution:** 3.25% of Gross Wages
- **Applicability:** If employee's gross wages ≤ ₹21,000/month
- **ESI Benefits:** Medical, sickness, maternity, disablement, and dependent benefits
- **ESIC Period:** April–September and October–March contribution periods

---

## Professional Tax (PT)

- **Threshold:** Monthly gross payable > ₹12,000
- **Deduction:** ₹200/month
- **No PT:** If monthly gross ≤ ₹12,000
- **Governed by:** Respective State Acts (amounts may vary by state)

---

## Overtime (OT) Pay Calculation

```
Extra Pay = (Per Day Rate ÷ 8) × Extra Duty Hours
```

- **Eligibility:** Employees who work beyond their scheduled shift hours
- **Maximum OT:** As per Factories Act — maximum 50 hours per quarter overtime (with government permission)
- **Double OT:** On national holidays, applicable employees receive double rate

---

## Paid Days Calculation

```
Paid Days = Present Days + Public Holidays + Weekly Off Days
```

- Absent days are not counted for paid days
- LOP days are deducted from paid days calculation

---

## Net Pay Formula

```
Net Pay = Total Payable Salary − EE PF − EE ESI − PT − LWF
Total Payable Salary = Total Earning − Other Deduction − Mediclaim − Shoes/Uniform
Total Earning = Salary + Extra Pay + Bin Card Amount + Difference Amount − Hours Deduction Amount
```

## Payslip Release

- Payslips are generated on payroll processing date
- Payslips are available for download via the system (PDF)
- Bulk payslip ZIP download available for HR

---

## Salary Revision

- Salary revisions are effective from the 1st of the revised month
- Mid-month revisions are pro-rated based on paid days
- Revisions require HR approval and updated employee master record

---

## Full & Final Settlement (FnF)

- Processed within 45 days of last working day
- Includes: Earned leave encashment, gratuity (if eligible), balance salary, PF withdrawal initiation
- Gratuity: Eligible after 5 years of continuous service [(Basic/26) × 15 × Years of Service]
